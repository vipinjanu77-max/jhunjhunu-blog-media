Editorial policy

Accuracy

We check facts against official or primary sources before publishing. Useful-information items show their source and the date we last checked them.

What we label

  • Interview: a guest's own account, in their words. We check factual claims where we can, and say when we couldn't.
  • Opinion: the author's view, clearly marked.
  • Sponsored: paid for by a business and labelled wherever it appears.

Editing interviews

We edit for length and clarity, but we never change what a guest meant, cut an answer so it says the opposite, or invent quotes.

Independence

Sponsors and partners have no say over our editorial decisions. Paying never buys a positive story. We do not publish political promotion as editorial content. If we have a personal or business connection to someone we cover, we say so.

People and privacy

We get consent from the people we feature, and a parent's consent for anyone under 18. We do not publish private details such as home addresses.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we correct it openly. See our corrections page.
